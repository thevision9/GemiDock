# Privacy Policy for GemiDock

Last updated: August 2026

GemiDock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles your data.

## Data Collection & Usage
* **No Personal Data Collection:** GemiDock does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user data.
* **100% Local Storage:** All folder configurations, subfolder hierarchies, color preferences, and prompt templates created within GemiDock are stored exclusively on your local device using standard Chrome local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
* **No Remote Servers:** GemiDock does not communicate with external servers or third-party analytics services.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/thevision9/GemiDock
